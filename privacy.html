Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Purpose

SaveReports Reader is a private, single-user tool. It uploads publicly available report files selected by the owner to a designated folder in the owner's Google Drive.

Google user data

The application uses Google Drive access solely to create folders and upload, list, or update files required for this private workflow. It does not sell, rent, share, or use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted repository secrets. Uploaded files remain in the owner's Google Drive account and are governed by the owner's Google Drive sharing settings.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and may delete uploaded files, revoke the application's Google access, or delete the application credentials at any time.

Contact

Questions about this private application may be sent to the support email displayed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
