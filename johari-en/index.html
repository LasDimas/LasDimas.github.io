Johari Window Questionnaire

20 workplace situations exploring how openly you share information and how willing you are to receive input from others.

Your answers and results stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

How to answer

For each question, you have 5 points to divide between options A and B. Give more points to the option that better reflects what you would do.

Move the slider between A and B. The closer it is to an option, the more points that option receives. The total always equals 5.

The Johari Window shows how we exchange information with others: how willing we are to share what we know and think, and how open we are to input from other people.

The higher both scores are, the larger the Arena - the area of open interaction.

The 0-100 scale is non-linear, so the distance between neighboring marks is not equal. This is not a good/bad rating; it is a prompt for reflection and discussion.
